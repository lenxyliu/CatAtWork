## Preconditions
Use a frame whose visible body crosses a fixed horizontal slot.
## Reproduction
Extract and render wide/tall poses with the former slot cutter.
## Expected
All fur and limbs remain inside a transparent safe canvas without chroma fringe.
## Actual
Edges turn green or body parts are cropped.
## Root cause
Fixed horizontal slots split silhouettes spanning slot boundaries.
## Impact
Permanent production-image damage and distracting runtime artifacts.
