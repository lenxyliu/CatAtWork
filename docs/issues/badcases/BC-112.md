# BC-112 — Interaction direction flips during an action

- Status: device-pending
- ISSUE: ISSUE-112
- First observed: before 2026-07-23
- Affected versions: legacy direction sampling
- Fixed versions: 2026-07-23 frozen workspace claim
- Regression test: gesture direction remains locked until action completion
- Automated evidence: frozen ISSUE-CLASSIFICATION section 3
- Device evidence: pending per-region left/right capture

## Preconditions
Begin an interaction with the pointer on one side and move during animation.
## Reproduction
Let the former renderer recompute direction every pointer sample.
## Expected
The recognized gesture owns a stable facing direction for its action.
## Actual
The body could flip even though the gesture began on the opposite side.
## Root cause
Direction was sampled continuously instead of captured in the intent.
## Impact
Direction jumps and wrong left/right response.
## Status timeline
- 2026-07-23: migrated; direction-lock code claim retained, device acceptance pending.
